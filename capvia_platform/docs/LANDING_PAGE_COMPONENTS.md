# CAPVIA Landing Page Components

## Directory Structure
`frontend/src/features/landing/components/`

## Component Inventory

1. **`Navbar`**: Fixed navigation with glassmorphism, brand logo, and auth links.
2. **`Hero`**: High-impact headline, subheadline, CTA buttons, and a dynamic hero graphic/dashboard preview.
3. **`Companies`**: Trusted by section featuring logos of enterprise clients.
4. **`Stats`**: Key performance metrics (e.g., millions of interviews, success rates).
5. **`Timeline`**: Step-by-step visual guide of the hiring process.
6. **`Comparison`**: Side-by-side comparison (Before CAPVIA vs. With CAPVIA).
7. **`AtsSection`**: Integration showcase for major ATS platforms.
8. **`Simulation`**: Interactive preview of the AI simulation environment.
9. **`Interview`**: Showcase of the AI interview capabilities.
10. **`IntegritySection`**: Details on anti-cheat and proctoring features.
11. **`DnaSection`**: Visual representation of the skills DNA profiling.
12. **`DashboardPreviews`**: High-fidelity mockups of recruiter and candidate dashboards.
13. **`Advantages`**: Grid of key benefits and features.
14. **`SecuritySection`**: Enterprise-grade security compliance and badges.
15. **`PricingSection`**: Clear, tiered pricing cards.
16. **`Testimonials`**: Social proof from enterprise clients.
17. **`FaqSection`**: Expandable accordion for common questions.
18. **`FinalCta`**: Bottom-of-page call to action to maximize conversions.
19. **`Footer`**: Comprehensive footer with links, legal, and social icons.
