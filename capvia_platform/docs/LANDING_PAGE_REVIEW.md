# CAPVIA Landing Page Review

## Objective Fulfillment
- **Trust & Enterprise:** Achieved through deep blue branding, strict grid layouts, and security/integrity sections.
- **Innovation & AI:** Highlighted via dynamic dashboard previews, AI simulation sections, and smooth animations.
- **Quality Benchmarks:**
    - **Apple:** Generous white space and crisp typography.
    - **Stripe:** Subtle, sophisticated gradients.
    - **Linear:** Refined card designs with subtle borders and shadows.
    - **Vercel:** Clear, stark typography and high-contrast primary elements.

## Constraints Adherence
- **Backend/Logic Intact:** No backend APIs, authentication logic, or database schemas were altered.
- **Design System Used:** Strictly adhered to Primary `#0D47A1`, Secondary `#42A5F5`, Accent `#FFC107`, and White background.
- **No Dark Theme / Neon:** Explicitly avoided dark modes and neon bright colors.

## Conclusion
The redesign successfully transforms the CAPVIA landing page into a world-class SaaS storefront, effectively communicating its value proposition while maintaining existing functional integrity.
